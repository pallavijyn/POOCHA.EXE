### Project Description
Poocha.exe is a mischievous virtual cat that disrupts your browsing experience by chasing your cursor, reacting to touches, making cat sounds, and sabotaging your searches. It turns a simple Google-style interface into a fun, interactive experience where the cat actively tries to stop you from being productive.
